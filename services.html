✓ OUR SERVICES

1. Building & Civil Engineering Construction

We undertake residential, commercial, institutional, and civil engineering construction projects, with emphasis on quality, durability, functionality, and proper project execution.

2. Building & Structural Consultancy

We provide professional consultancy services relating to building construction, structural works, construction methods, technical assessments, and project execution.

3. Architectural & Structural Designs

Professional building design and structural documentation in collaboration with relevant built-environment professionals. Includes 3D visualisation, working drawings, and construction details.

4. Quantity Surveying & Cost Management

Cost estimation, Bills of Quantities, budget preparation, tender documentation, valuation of work executed, cost control, final accounts, and material cost analysis.

5. Construction & Project Management

We coordinate and manage construction activities from project commencement to completion, ensuring proper planning, resource management, quality control, cost monitoring, and timely delivery.

6. Pre-Construction Services

Project feasibility assessment, preliminary cost estimates, design coordination, construction planning, budget development, procurement planning, and programme preparation.

7. Builders' Documentation

We prepare and coordinate relevant construction documentation required for effective project planning, including Program of Work (Construction program) and Health & Safety plans.

8. Renovation, Remodelling & Rehabilitation

We transform existing buildings to improve their functionality, appearance, structural performance, space utilisation, and overall value.

9. Office Build-outs & Expansion

We provide construction and fit-out solutions for offices, commercial spaces, institutional facilities, and other corporate environments.

10. Material Procurement & Construction Planning

We assist clients with strategic procurement, material selection, sourcing, scheduling, and cost-effective construction planning.

11. Real Estate Development

We provide construction and development support for property development projects, from planning and budgeting through construction and completion.

12. Sustainable Building Solutions

We promote construction approaches that improve energy efficiency, resource utilisation, durability, environmental responsibility, and long-term building performance.

13. Emergency Construction Services

We provide responsive construction and building-related services for urgent repairs, remedial works, structural concerns, and other emergency construction requirements.
